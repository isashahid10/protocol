01 Context

Robots cannot learn a job they have never seen done.

Internet video shows the end result. It rarely shows the grip on a screwdriver, the angle of a connector or the force it takes to seat a bearing. That is exactly the data manipulation models are starved of.

We capture it at the source. Skilled operators wear our rigs while they do their real jobs, in real workshops and on real production lines. Every stream is synced, every frame is annotated, and every dataset is built to your spec.

04 Annotation

All data is annotated. To an industrial standard.

Nothing ships raw unless you ask for it. Hands, objects, actions, gaze and force are labelled by trained annotators, checked by a second pass and signed off by a domain expert who knows the task. Every label is traceable to the frame it came from.

  • QA.1

    Two pass labelling

    Two annotators label blind. A domain expert adjudicates every disagreement before release.

  • QA.2

    Measured agreement

    Inter-annotator agreement is scored on every batch and published in the data card. We target κ ≥ 0.90.

  • QA.3

    Your label spec

    Start from our verb and noun action taxonomy and 21 point hand skeleton, or bring your own schema.

  • QA.4

    Stream level QC

    Sync drift, dropped frames, occlusion and sensor faults are flagged per clip. Nothing is silently dropped.

  • QA.5

    Open formats

    MCAP, HDF5, COCO keypoints, MP4 with JSON and LeRobot style datasets. Loads straight into your stack.

  • QA.6

    Privacy built in

    Faces, screens and badges are blurred. Each label carries annotator, timestamp and review history.

06 Uses

Who trains on avcon data.

  • A · World models

    World models

    First person video of real work, so models learn how objects respond to hands, tools and force.

  • B · Manipulation

    Robot manipulation policies

    Hand pose, grasp and force labels for imitation learning and pretraining dexterous arms.

  • C · VLA

    Vision language action models

    Narrated, segmented tasks that tie words like "seat the connector" to the exact motion.

  • D · Humanoids

    Humanoid robots

    Full upper body motion, bimanual hand pose and gaze for humanoids working in human spaces.

  • E · Evaluation

    Benchmarks

    Held out task suites that are never published, to test robot models on work they have not seen.

  • F · Industrial vision

    Step verification

    Train systems that check each assembly step and catch mistakes on real production lines.

07 Process

From a task description to a training set.

  1. 01

    Scope

    You tell us the task, the environment and the streams you need. We agree a label spec and volume.

  2. 02

    Sample

    We send annotated sample clips first, so your team can judge the data before committing.

  3. 03

    Capture

    Skilled operators are fitted and calibrated, then recorded doing real work in our partner sites or your facility.

  4. 04

    Annotate

    Two pass labelling, expert sign off and stream level QC on every clip.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Batches with a data card, shipped to your bucket or pulled from our API on your schedule.

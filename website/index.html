01 Context

Satellites see the surface. We measure what is underneath.

Earth observation gives you pixels. It does not give you soil moisture at 30 cm, the nitrate load in a river after a storm, or the dawn chorus in a forest that is coming back. That gap is where environmental models drift.

We close it the slow way. Instruments in the ground, calibrated against reference standards, recording every minute through every season. The result is labelled, gap checked and ready to train on.

04 Uses

Who trains on avcon data.

  • A · Climate

    Weather and climate models

    Station grade observations to downscale gridded forecasts, correct bias and validate reanalysis where the network is thin.

  • B · Agriculture

    Precision agriculture

    Soil and canopy truth for yield, irrigation scheduling and early disease detection. Labelled by agronomists, not guessed.

  • C · MRV

    Carbon and nature MRV

    Defensible measurement, reporting and verification for carbon, water and biodiversity outcomes, with an audit trail per reading.

  • D · Biodiversity

    Species detection AI

    Thousands of hours of labelled field audio and camera frames, covering birds, bats, amphibians, insects and the machines that disturb them.

  • E · Evaluation

    Benchmarks for earth models

    Held out field data, never published, to test geospatial foundation models against what really happened on the ground.

  • F · Cities

    Urban heat and air

    Street level temperature and pollution at block resolution for heat action plans, insurers and city digital twins.

05 Field to model

From a patch of ground to a training set.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We pick sites with landholders and local partners, map the terrain and agree what is measured and who owns it.

  2. 02

    Deploy

    Stations go in within a day. Sensors are bench calibrated before they leave and checked against reference units on site.

  3. 03

    Record

    Every channel is time synced and logged at the edge, then sent home over LoRa, cellular or satellite.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Drift correction, outlier flags and gap reports run on every batch. Experts label audio, imagery and events.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Clean tables, raw streams and a data card, shipped to your bucket or pulled from our API on your schedule.

    06 Ethos

    Ecocentric means the land comes first.

    We measure a place without taking from it. These are not slogans, they are rules our field teams work by.

    • I

      Light footprint

      Solar powered, no concrete footings where we can avoid it, and a site left as we found it when a deployment ends.

    • II

      Hardware comes home

      Stations are built to be recovered, repaired and redeployed. Batteries and boards go back into the loop, not into the ground.

    • III

      Consent from the ground up

      Landholders and communities are paid partners. They see their own data, and sensitive species locations are never published.

    • IV

      An open baseline

      A share of every network is released for public research, so the people protecting a place can use what we learn there.
